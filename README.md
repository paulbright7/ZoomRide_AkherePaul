# ZoomRide_AkherePaul
## 📊Project Overveiw
This project analyzes a transportation company called ZoomRide operation across six cities to understand the performance across these cities and to know where necessary adjustment are needed to be made
This project is also part of my data analytics portfolio showcasing Exploratory Data Analysis(EDA) Skills in MySQL.

## 💡Business Question Answered

- Which month of the year generate more revenue
- which vehicle generate more revenue
- Top 3 Customers by Total Money Spend

## 🔍Key Insight
- The sum of ₦568,470 was recorded as total revenue
- Out of the 6 cities, Lagos generated the most revenue (₦218,890) and most completed trips (93)
- There are 9 completed trips with missing fare
- Out of 40 customers, 4 customers haven`t book a ride before
- December is the most demand month when it comes to booking a ride
- Economy Vehicle generated most most revenue (₦262,550) with 121 completed trips 
- There were two duplicated trips which i removed

## 📍Conclusion
From the insight gotten above, we can see that Lagos generated 38% in revenue and completed 93 trips. I will advice that the company invest more in Lagos city but before doing so, the company should look into the average fare in each city.
